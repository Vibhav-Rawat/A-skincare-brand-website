A gentle reminder

Introduce new products gradually, patch test when appropriate, and stop using a product if it causes irritation. Skincare needs vary; this guide is general information, not medical advice.

GOOD TO KNOW

Quick questions

Do I need a complicated routine?

No. Many people start with a gentle cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen in the morning.

Can I try several new products at once?

It's usually easier to introduce one new product at a time so you can notice how your skin responds.

Is sunscreen just for sunny days?

Sun protection is useful as part of a daytime routine. Follow the product label and reapply as directed.
