OUR LITTLE STORY

Small steps can
feel ethereal.

VIBHAVÉ was inspired by the following principle: Skincare must be easy, not complicated. We wanted to create a simple line of products where each one had a specific function and an inspiring design.

Concept brand that was created by me lmao, which stresses the importance of simplicity, accessibility, and quiet time for oneself in a hectic schedule.
